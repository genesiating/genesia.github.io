Welcome!

|

Design, if applied appropriately, can be a catalyst for social good. My expertise is innovating within largely "bureaucratic" spaces with a laser focus on social impact and civic tech. At present, I'm designing products at Recidiviz, a non-profit focused on criminal justice reform.

Before that, I pushed pixels at the Los Angeles Times, Census Open Innovation Labs, Mapbox, EchoUser (a small UX design firm), Airbel Impact Lab, International Rescue Committee, Almost Fun SAT, Coding it Forward, Pivot to Civic, and more.

My design ethos is loosely woven from my experiences co-designing with brave 911 emergency dispatchers, tenacious newsroom journalists, resilient incarcerated individuals, and dedicated public servants.

If you're in a position to give, I'd love to invite anyone reading this to support a few causes I care about – or alternatively, to any local organizations that are meaningful to you.
